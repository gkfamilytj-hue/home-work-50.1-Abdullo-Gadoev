Here to help

Find every answer you need right here.

Explore common questions about plans, appointments, and getting started with Mecare.
01. What services do you offer?

We provide a range of healthcare services, including primary care, specialist consultations, preventive screenings, diagnostic testing, and telehealth.

02. How can I book an appointment?

Contact our care team by phone or email, and we will help you find a convenient time and the right specialist for your needs.

03. Do you offer telehealth services?

Many consultations can be arranged remotely. Our care team can help you check whether a telehealth appointment is right for your visit.

04. What is your cancellation policy?

Please contact us as early as possible if you need to change your appointment. Our team will help you reschedule and explain any applicable notice requirements.

05. What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring a photo ID, insurance information if applicable, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records or test results.

06. How do I get my test results?

Your care team will let you know when and how to expect your results. If you have questions, contact the team that arranged your tests.

07. Can I refill my prescription online?

Contact your pharmacy or care team to request a refill and confirm whether a follow-up visit is needed.

08. How do I contact my doctor after hours?

For urgent matters after hours, contact our on-call physician through the main phone line. For non-urgent questions, leave a message and we will respond during regular business hours.

09. What should I do if I have an emergency?

If you think you may be experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

10. How can I access my medical records online?

Contact our care team to learn how to access your records securely and get help with your online account.
